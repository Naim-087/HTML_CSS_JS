About MediReminder

MediReminder is a web-based medicine reminder system developed to help users manage their daily medications efficiently and reduce the chances of missing important doses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make medicine management simple, organized, and accessible for everyone.

Key Features

User Account

Create an account and securely manage your medicine schedule.

Medicine Management

Add, edit, delete, and organize medicines with ease.

Reminder Tracking

Receive reminders and monitor your medicine-taking history.
