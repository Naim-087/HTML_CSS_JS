About MediReminder

A simple desktop medicine reminder and management system.

Project Goal

MediReminder helps users organize medicines, maintain daily medication schedules, and reduce missed doses through a simple and user-friendly desktop application.

Key Features

👤 User Account

Register and login securely.

💊 Medicine Management

Add, view, delete and manage medicines.

📅 Medicine History

Track medicines marked as taken.
